Getting started

How do I create my first scorecard?

Sign up, then pick a template from the Templates page or start from scratch in the builder. Edit the questions and scores, then publish. Publishing gives you a link you can share right away.

Where do I share my scorecard?

Anywhere you talk to customers: WhatsApp status and chats, Instagram bio, ad links, email. The builder has a Share on WhatsApp button and gives you embed code if you want the scorecard inside your own website.

Can I see it the way my leads will?

Yes. Open your scorecard's live link, or try the Live Demo first to see the full flow.

Leads and results

Where do my leads go?

Every completed scorecard shows up on your Leads page with a score and a readiness tier. You also get an email when a new lead comes in, and you can export your leads to CSV.

What does the lead get?

A personal result on screen and a PDF report by email, written from their own answers. You can rewrite any of that text in the builder.

Can leads go straight into my CRM?

Yes. Add a webhook address in the builder and Sieve sends each new lead to it, signed so your system can check it came from Sieve. This works with tools like Zapier and Make.

Account and team

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password on the login page. We email you a reset link. It works once and expires after one hour.

How do I add my team?

Open Team in your dashboard and invite people by email. How many seats you get depends on your plan.

How do I close my account or delete my data?

Email results@sieve.com.ng from the email address on your account. See our Privacy Policy for what we delete and what the law makes us keep.

Billing

How do I pay?

All payments are in Naira through Paystack. You can buy prepaid credits, pay per lead with a saved card, or take a monthly plan. See Pricing.

What happens when my credits or plan limit run out?

Your dashboard warns you as you get close. When you run out, your scorecard stops taking new visitors until you top up. Anyone already in the middle of it can still finish.

I think I was charged wrongly.

Email results@sieve.com.ng with your account email and the Paystack reference, and we will look into it.
