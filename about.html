About Sieve

Sieve helps Nigerian businesses find out which leads are ready to buy, before anyone picks up the phone.

Why Sieve exists

Most Nigerian businesses get enquiries through WhatsApp, Instagram and paid ads. Many of those people are only browsing. Sales teams spend hours chatting with people who were never going to buy, while the serious buyers wait.

Sieve fixes that with a short scorecard: a quiz of a few questions that a lead answers in a couple of minutes. Every answer carries a score, so by the time a lead lands in your dashboard you already know how ready they are.

What Sieve does

  • Build a scorecard. Start from one of our ready-made templates for your industry, or write your own questions. You choose how each answer is scored.
  • Share it anywhere. Publish it as a link for WhatsApp, Instagram, ads or email, or embed it on your own website.
  • Give every lead something useful. Each person gets a personal result on screen, plus a PDF report by email.
  • See who is ready. Leads arrive in your dashboard with a score and a readiness tier. Export them to CSV, get an email for each new lead, or send them to your own tools by webhook.
  • See the bigger picture. A report shows completion rate, average score and how your audience is spread across tiers, without naming anyone.

Built for Nigeria
