For agencies and consultants

If you build marketing or sales systems for clients, Sieve already has what you need to run it for them:

  • Your client's name, not ours. Each scorecard can show its own brand name, so the results page, PDF and email all carry your client's name.
  • Team seats. Invite teammates to the same account. The number of seats depends on your plan.
  • Embed and webhooks. Put a scorecard inside your client's website and send each new lead straight into their CRM or automation tool.
  • Brand-ready reports. Share an audience report that shows totals and charts, with no names or personal answers.

To apply to be listed, email results@sieve.com.ng with your business name, where you are based, and one or two scorecards you have built on Sieve.
