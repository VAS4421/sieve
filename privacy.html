Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how VASNET Technologies Limited ("VASNET", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you use Sieve at sieve.com.ng. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2019 (NDPR) where it still applies, and guidance from the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

1. Who we are

VASNET Technologies Limited, 3A Alapere, Lagos. For any privacy question or request, email results@sieve.com.ng.

2. Two kinds of people, two roles

  • Sieve customers are businesses and people who sign up to build scorecards. For their account data, VASNET is the data controller.
  • Respondents are people who take a scorecard that a customer has published. The customer decides what to ask and what to do with the answers, so the customer is the data controller and VASNET is the data processor acting on the customer's instructions. If you took a scorecard and want your data changed or removed, contact the business that shared it with you. You can also email us and we will pass your request on and help them act on it.

3. What we collect

From customers

  • Account details: name, business name, email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Team details: the email addresses of teammates you invite.
  • What you build: scorecards, questions, wording, images and logos you upload, and settings such as webhook addresses.
  • Billing records: your plan, credit balance and payment history. Card payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your full card number. For pay-per-lead, Paystack gives us a reusable card token so it can charge you; it is useless to anyone outside Paystack.

From respondents (on behalf of customers)

  • Name, email address and, if the business asks for it, phone number.
  • Answers to the scorecard, the score and tier those answers produce, and how long it took to finish.
  • If the business turns it on: age range, gender, state of residence, income bracket and one interest question, always chosen from fixed options.
  • If you start a scorecard and leave without finishing, your name and email may be kept so the business can send you one reminder email.

Technical data

  • Basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) kept to run the service, stop abuse and fix errors.

4. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide Sieve (accounts, scorecards, scoring, results, PDF reports, emails, dashboards): to perform our contract with customers.
  • To take payment and keep financial records: contract, and our legal duties under Nigerian tax and company law.
  • To keep Sieve secure (rate limits, login protection, error logs): our legitimate interest in a safe service.
  • To send service emails (password resets, team invites, new-lead alerts, billing notices): contract.
  • Respondent data: processed only on the customer's instructions. Each customer is responsible for having a lawful basis, usually consent, to collect it.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use respondent data to market to respondents ourselves. We do not run advertising trackers on Sieve.

5. Cookies

Sieve uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It is needed for the service to work and is not used for tracking or advertising. Your browser may also store small preferences on your own device.

6. Who we share data with

  • The business that owns the scorecard sees the respondent data for their own scorecards.
  • Service providers who help us run Sieve, under contract and only for that purpose: our cloud hosting and database provider, our email delivery provider, and Paystack for payments.
  • Places a customer chooses: if a customer sets up a webhook, new leads are sent to the address they provide. That is the customer's decision and responsibility.
  • Authorities, when Nigerian law or a valid court order requires it.

7. Data stored outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Nigeria. When that happens we rely on the grounds allowed in Part VIII of the NDPA, such as adequate protection in the receiving country, contractual safeguards, or the transfer being needed to perform our contract with you.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data: for as long as your account is open, then deleted within 90 days of closure, except what we must keep by law.
  • Respondent data: for as long as the customer keeps the scorecard or the lead. When a customer deletes a scorecard, its leads go with it.
  • Payment and tax records: for as long as Nigerian law requires (generally six years).
  • Server and error logs: kept for a limited time, only as long as needed for security and fixing problems.

9. How we protect data

Sieve runs over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, password-reset and invite links expire and work once, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, every customer can only reach their own scorecards and leads, and webhook messages are signed. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell affected people without undue delay, as the NDPA requires.

10. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, or limit how we use it;
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interest;
  • send your data to you or another provider in a common format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

Email results@sieve.com.ng from the address linked to your data. We may need to confirm who you are. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

11. Children

Sieve accounts are for people aged 18 and over. Customers must not use Sieve to collect data from children without the consent of a parent or guardian, as the NDPA requires.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change matters, we will also tell customers by email or in the dashboard before it takes effect.
